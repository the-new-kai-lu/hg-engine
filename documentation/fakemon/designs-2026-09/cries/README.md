# Cry drafts

Open [listen.html](listen.html) for eleven custom drafts and all 36 Gen 1–4 starter-stage references and 36 additional comparison cries, with filters, individual/sequence playback, volume and WAV downloads.

Reproduce with `.venv/bin/python scripts/generate_fakemon_cries.py`. Original oscillator/FM/noise voices; varan revision 3 also mixes CC0 fireplace and snowstorm recordings (see SOURCES.md). Existing Pokemon cries are not sampled into the new designs. Voltuff retains its original chatter. Electric revision 3 uses sustained, bright FM screeches over a quieter vocal body; Raijinque adds tonal buzzing, rapid pitch steps and a descending discharge rather than random-noise crackles. Earlier versions remain under Electric v1 and Electric v2. Luxray/Electivire are listening references, not sampled into the generated cries. Embernewt retains its accepted single call. Varan revision 3 uses independently shaped evolved calls with real fireplace crackles or gusting snowstorm texture; v1/v2 remain archived. Bird revision 5 preserves Sedgling. Cragaviar adds a strained, bright rasp and fast irregular interruptions; Ragnaroc gains stronger midrange quake harmonics and loudness calibrated against Pidgeot/Staraptor. Prior bird revisions are archived.

Reference WAVs are copied unchanged from sound/cries; retail provenance has not been independently verified. Nothing is assigned to gameplay yet. Electric revision 3 and the full varan family revision 3 are accepted. Bird revision 5 is approved; all eleven current cries are now approved and awaiting engine installation.
