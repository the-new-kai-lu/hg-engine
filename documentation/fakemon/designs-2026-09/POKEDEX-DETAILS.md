## Size rationale

The small forms are around knee height. The electric progression remains exactly as printed. Both varan branches have equal listed heights at matching stages and substantial final-stage mass, reflecting their broad, armored quadrupedal bodies. The Ice branch is modestly heavier to account for solid crown/shoulder ice where the Fire branch has flame. This is a chosen design interpretation, not a physical calculation of material density.

Bird heights describe their standing body/head height, not the reach of raised wings. Ragnaroc is the tallest of the three final silhouettes, with a broad four-winged outline and a substantial but lighter body than the varans. Cragaviar and Ragnaroc carry more mass than their slim necks alone suggest because of the stony plumage. Varan heights use their illustrated upright-neck stance; tails and transient flame tips are not added to height. No wingspan or body-length fields are invented for the engine.

## Entry text

Source: complete text supplied by the user; all eleven entries are authoritative.

### Voltuff

They brush against one another to build up electricity. Together, they create dazzling showers of sparks that frighten off much larger Pokémon.

### Surguenon

Troop members practice together until their movements match perfectly. Their synchronized strikes overwhelm foes with powerful electric shocks.

### Raijinque

The thunder of its four fists can be heard beyond the mountains. Villagers once believed that storms began wherever these Pokémon gathered to train.

### Embernewt

Its will to live is remarkably strong. As its body begins to freeze, it draws its flame deep inside to keep it burning.

### Pyrovaran

It basks on warm rocks along the slopes of volcanoes. As it matures, it makes its home ever closer to the crater.

### Magmalisk

Volcanic eruptions were once believed to be the result of two Magmalisk fighting over territory deep beneath the mountain. Its every step leaves a pool of bubbling lava behind.

### Rimevaran

Only an Embernewt whose flame survives being sealed in ice becomes this Pokémon. It draws all warmth from the surrounding air to keep its inner flame alive.

### Fimbulisk

Travelers once froze solid following its glowing eyes through blizzards, believing them to be a distant hearth. Where it rests, winter lingers long after the surrounding snow has melted.

### Sedgling

It loves to nap in shallow puddles. Its down soaks up so much water that it sometimes wakes to find itself sitting on dry ground.

### Cragaviar

It soaks its stone-like feathers in rivers before long flights. The absorbed water binds their layers together, repairing cracks caused by wear.

### Ragnaroc

Ancient accounts describe a four-winged shadow followed by a rain of falling stars. Buried settlements have been found beneath the paths of its migrations.
