# Retired Electric / Fighting feline line

Superseded by the user-supplied Voltuff, Embernewt and Sedgling concept sheets. This directory is an archive only; its design approvals no longer apply to the active project. See [the current project](../../README.md).

## Confirmed design
- Three stages; names are undecided.
- Stage 1: pure Electric; upright feline with relaxed paws and a non-fighting stance.
- Stages 2 and 3: Electric/Fighting, with martial poses.
- Stages 1 and 2 have two arms; stage 3 has four.
- Final form has two solid fighting arms and two amorphous purple thundercloud arms ending in clearly visible solid fists. Cloud arms should not look muscular.
- Lean, simplified feline bodies with straighter torsos, reduced muscle definition and limited fur detail.
- Cream angular neck mane grows from a small collar in stage 1 to a medium ruff in stage 2 and fuller mane in stage 3.
- Gold, cream, charcoal and purple palette; lightning markings; pointed ears, feline faces and cloud-tipped tails.
- Inspirations: Meowth (feline base), Blaziken (martial stance/proportions), Machamp (four-arm final form), Raikou (electric/cloud motifs).
- User suggested pokemondb.net/pokedex/[name] for additional references.
